SKZ Biography

Step Out. Stray Kids.

This is a brief biography of the South Korean boy group, Stray Kids (SKZ).

Hi there!, you must be a Stay! or want to know more about them and their origins. Then lets explore their journey together.

Stray Kids is a South Korean boy group formed by JYP Entertainment in 2017. The group consists of eight members: Bang Chan, Lee Know, Changbin, Hyunjin, Han, Felix, Seungmin, and I.N. They debuted on March 25, 2018, with their extended play (EP) "I Am NOT."

I.N: The Maknae

I.N, whose real name is Yang Jeongin, is the youngest member of Stray Kids. Born on February 8, 2001, in Busan, South Korea.

Before his debut, he honed his skills by singing trot music, which gave him a unique vocal texture.

"I will always try my best to be a person you can be proud of."

Seungmin: The Vocalist

Kim Seungmin, born September 22, 2000, is the group's main vocal pillar. Known for his disciplined work ethic and "Dandy Boy" image.

He is widely praised for his clear, stable live vocals and his ability to convey deep emotion.

"If I stay still, I won't be able to go anywhere. That's why I keep moving."

Felix: The Visual

Lee Jinho, known by his stage name Felix, is the visual of Stray Kids. Born on February 18, 2001, in Seoul, South Korea.

He is known for his charming looks and expressive performances, often described as having a "cute" and "charming" appearance.

"I want to be someone who can make people smile."

Han: The Producer

Han Jisung, known mononymously as Han, is the main rapper and producer of Stray Kids. Born on September 14, 2000, in Incheon, South Korea.

He is recognized for his exceptional songwriting and producing skills, contributing significantly to the group's music.

"I want to create music that can touch people's hearts."

Hyunjin: The Dancer

Hwang Hyunjin, born March 20, 2000, is the main dancer and lead rapper of Stray Kids. Known for his charismatic stage presence and powerful dance skills.

He is often praised for his versatility in dance styles and his ability to captivate audiences with his performances.

"I want to be a performer who can express emotions through dance."

Changbin: The Lyricist

Seo Changbin, born August 11, 1999, is the main rapper and lyricist of Stray Kids. Known for his deep voice and powerful rap delivery.

He is highly regarded for his lyrical prowess, often writing meaningful and impactful lyrics for the group's songs.

"I want to write lyrics that can inspire and resonate with people."

Lee Know: The Main Dancer

Lee Minho, known by his stage name Lee Know, is the main dancer and lead vocalist of Stray Kids. Born on October 25, 1998, in Gimpo, South Korea.

He is celebrated for his exceptional dance skills and his ability to convey emotion through his performances.

"I want to be a dancer who can tell stories through movement."

Bang Chan: The Leader

Bang Chan, born October 3, 1997, is the leader and main producer of Stray Kids. Known for his strong leadership skills and musical talent.

He is highly respected for his dedication to the group and his ability to create music that resonates with fans.

"I want to lead Stray Kids to become a group that can inspire and connect with people through music."

The Legacy

With eight members—Bang Chan, Lee Know, Changbin, Hyunjin, Han, Felix, Seungmin, and I.N—Stray Kids continues to break records globally. Their connection with their fans, known as "Stay," is the driving force behind their "Stray Kids Everywhere All Around The World" mission.
